### Hi, I'm Mohammad Arham 👋

Naval architecture student at **NUST** (class of 2027), based in Karachi. I work on hull design, intact stability and resistance for small craft and unmanned surface vessels (USVs), using Rhino 3D, Python and JavaScript.

#### 🚢 Featured project: [HullForge](https://mianarhamm.github.io/hullforge/)
Parametric catamaran and monohull design in the browser: lines plan, hydrostatics, GZ curve with IMO criteria, and Michell thin-ship resistance, with DXF / CSV / STL exports.
Validated against **MAXSURF**: hydrostatics and GZ within 0.3 %. → [code](https://github.com/MianArhamm/hullforge) · [live app](https://mianarhamm.github.io/hullforge/)

#### 🔬 Research
Final-year project: a hydrographic survey USV catamaran. I modelled ten hulls from the Southampton NPL and Series 64 catamaran series in Rhino, compared their stability and resistance, and selected a 1.2 m Series 64 prototype for fabrication.

#### 🛠️ Tools
Rhino 3D · MAXSURF · Python (NumPy, SciPy) · JavaScript · three.js · CAD for fabrication (DXF, STEP, STL)
